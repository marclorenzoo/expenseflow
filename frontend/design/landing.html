Features

Everything you need to split fairly

From the first invite to the final settle-up, ExpenseFlow keeps shared money clear and drama-free.

Create groups & invite

Spin up a group for any trip, flat or event and invite friends with a single link — no sign-up friction.

Split any way

Divide equally, by exact amounts, shares or percentages — across multiple currencies, all converted for you.

Who owes who

Insights & analytics
